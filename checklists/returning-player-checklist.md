# Star Trek Online Returning Player Checklist

A compact companion to the full [Returning Player Guide](../guides/returning-player-guide.md).

**Last reviewed:** September 2026  
**Platform focus:** PC, with general steps for console

## Before You Play

- [ ] Update the launcher and game.
- [ ] Confirm your account email and security settings.
- [ ] Read current news for your platform.
- [ ] Check active events, deadlines, maintenance, and giveaways.
- [ ] Log in to your established captain before creating a replacement.

## Protect Your Account Progress

- [ ] Review every character.
- [ ] Review ships and account unlocks.
- [ ] Check inventory, bank, account bank, mail, and overflow.
- [ ] Keep unfamiliar or old items until researched.
- [ ] Check reclaim stores and event rewards.
- [ ] Record current equipment, traits, and abilities.
- [ ] Save the current loadout before making changes.

## Relearn the Controls

- [ ] Check movement and camera settings.
- [ ] Review targeting and auto-attack settings.
- [ ] Rebuild the ability tray.
- [ ] Review keybinds or controller settings.
- [ ] Check power presets.
- [ ] Identify heals, firing modes, and movement abilities.
- [ ] Run an easy story mission or normal patrol.

## Choose One Captain and Ship

- [ ] Select one captain for the first week.
- [ ] Choose one ship you enjoy.
- [ ] Define the ship's role.
- [ ] Choose one main damage source.
- [ ] Confirm the ship supports the chosen plan.
- [ ] Delay work on other characters until this build is stable.

## Repair the Build

- [ ] Align weapons with the damage plan.
- [ ] Remove consoles which do not support the plan.
- [ ] Review bridge-officer abilities.
- [ ] Add cooldown support.
- [ ] Add dependable healing and resistance.
- [ ] Review power levels.
- [ ] Select personal, reputation, and starship traits.
- [ ] Review active-space duty officers.
- [ ] Confirm primary and secondary specializations.
- [ ] Test before upgrading equipment.

## Review Progression

- [ ] Check reputation progress and stored marks.
- [ ] Review Endeavors.
- [ ] Restart selected Admiralty assignments.
- [ ] Review duty officer assignments.
- [ ] Check R&D schools, projects, and materials.
- [ ] Review episode and mission progress.
- [ ] Choose only the systems you want to resume now.

## Protect Scarce Resources

Delay major spending during your first week.

- [ ] Zen
- [ ] Lobi
- [ ] Keys
- [ ] Ship coupons or choice packs
- [ ] Dilithium
- [ ] Energy Credits
- [ ] Fleet Credits and provisions
- [ ] Upgrade tokens and technology upgrades
- [ ] Event reward choices

Before spending, confirm the purchase has a defined purpose, works with the build, and lacks a suitable owned or earned alternative.

## Return to Team Content

- [ ] Start on normal difficulty.
- [ ] Read the mission objective.
- [ ] Clear injuries or ship damage where relevant.
- [ ] Bring a dependable heal.
- [ ] Stay with the group.
- [ ] Follow objective prompts.
- [ ] Ask when a mechanic is unclear.
- [ ] Move to advanced content only after the build feels stable.

## Seven-Session Plan

- [ ] Session 1: Audit the account and inventory.
- [ ] Session 2: Rebuild controls and run easy content.
- [ ] Session 3: Choose the ship's role and damage plan.
- [ ] Session 4: Align equipment, abilities, traits, and power.
- [ ] Session 5: Review progression systems.
- [ ] Session 6: Run normal team content.
- [ ] Session 7: Choose the next three improvements.

## When Asking for Help

Prepare:

- [ ] Platform
- [ ] Captain faction, career, and level
- [ ] Ship name and tier
- [ ] Weapons and equipment
- [ ] Consoles
- [ ] Bridge-officer abilities
- [ ] Traits and duty officers
- [ ] Specializations
- [ ] Preferred play style
- [ ] Target difficulty
- [ ] Available budget
- [ ] Screenshots or a build-planner link
