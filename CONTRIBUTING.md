# Contributing to SSC Fleet Resources

Thank you for helping improve SSC Fleet resources.

## Ways to Contribute

- Report outdated information.
- Suggest a new guide or checklist.
- Correct spelling, broken links, or unclear wording.
- Submit tested Star Trek Online advice.
- Improve accessibility and formatting.

## Submission Standards

Contributions should be respectful, original, and written in clear language. State the game platform when advice differs between PC and console. Separate confirmed information from personal preference.

Do not submit:

- Private member information
- Account credentials
- Datamined or confidential material
- Harassment or discriminatory content
- Copied guides without permission
- Unapproved use of the SSC logo

## Review

SSC maintainers review proposed changes before publication. Submission does not guarantee acceptance. Material might be edited for accuracy, tone, formatting, or consistency.

For general fleet discussion, use the [SSC Fleet Forum](https://starfleetstrategiccommand.com/ssc-forum/).
