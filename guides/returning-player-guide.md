# Returning Player Guide

Star Trek Online changes over time. Start by reviewing your account and learning the current systems before replacing equipment or spending premium currency.

## Account Review

- Confirm your characters, ships, currencies, and account unlocks.
- Check reclaim stores for previously earned rewards.
- Review active events and their remaining time.
- Inspect traits, specializations, duty officers, and bridge-officer abilities.
- Save screenshots of older builds before changing them.

## First Sessions Back

1. Use normal-difficulty patrols or missions to relearn controls.
2. Review your power levels, ability tray, and keybinds.
3. Check whether your equipment supports one main damage type.
4. Join a normal TFO after your controls feel familiar.
5. Ask for advice before buying a new ship or equipment pack.

## Spending Priorities

Avoid rushing into purchases. A stronger build often starts with matching weapons, suitable tactical consoles, dependable bridge-officer abilities, and a clear role.

## Useful Information to Share

When asking for help, state:

- How long you have been away
- Your platform
- Your character career
- Your current ship
- Your preferred content
- Whether you prefer free, earned, or paid options

Visit the [SSC Fleet website](https://starfleetstrategiccommand.com/) for current articles and fleet information.
