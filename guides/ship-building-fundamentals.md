# Ship-Building Fundamentals

A dependable ship build starts with a clear purpose. Choose how the ship deals damage or supports the team, then select equipment and abilities serving that purpose.

## Choose a Main Approach

Common starting approaches include:

- Energy weapons with beams
- Energy weapons with cannons
- Torpedoes
- Exotic damage
- Tanking
- Team support

New players usually benefit from focusing on one main approach before combining several systems.

## Build Foundations

### Weapons

Use a consistent energy type where practical. Matching damage types makes tactical-console choices easier.

### Consoles

Choose consoles supporting your primary damage source, survivability needs, or ship mechanics. Avoid filling slots with unrelated bonuses.

### Bridge-Officer Abilities

Plan abilities as a complete rotation. Include attacks, cooldown support, healing, resistance, and control where needed.

### Traits and Duty Officers

Traits and duty officers should reinforce the same plan. Expensive options are not required for a functional starting build.

### Power and Movement

Energy-weapon ships need suitable weapon power. Positioning, speed, firing arcs, and distance affect results as much as equipment.

## Upgrade Order

1. Fix weapon and console conflicts.
2. Organize bridge-officer abilities.
3. Set power levels and controls.
4. Test in repeatable content.
5. Upgrade equipment after the build works together.
6. Change one major element at a time.

## Build Review Checklist

Share your ship, career, equipment, traits, bridge-officer seating, budget, and target content when requesting help.

A good build fits your resources and remains enjoyable to fly.
