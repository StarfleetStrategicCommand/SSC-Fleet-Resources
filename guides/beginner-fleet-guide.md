# Star Trek Online Beginner Fleet Guide

A practical guide to joining, understanding, and participating in a Star Trek Online fleet.

**Last reviewed:** September 2026  
**Platform focus:** PC, with general guidance for console fleets  
**Maintained by:** Starfleet Strategic Command, The Original SSC

> Fleet rules, ranks, stores, and contribution requirements differ between communities. Read a fleet's own policies before donating resources or requesting store access.

## 1. What a Fleet Provides

A fleet is a player-run community. The experience depends on its members, leadership, activity level, and goals.

A fleet might provide:

- Social support and answers to game questions
- Organized missions, Task Force Operations, and events
- Access to developed fleet holdings and stores
- Ship-building guidance
- Shared resources and community tools
- A forum, Discord server, or other communication channel
- Help for new and returning captains

Membership does not guarantee immediate access to every store or service. Many fleets use ranks, contribution requirements, or request procedures to manage limited provisions.

## 2. How to Choose a Fleet

Choose a fleet matching how you prefer to play. A large active fleet is not automatically a better fit than a smaller relaxed community.

Ask these questions before joining:

- Is the fleet active during your usual playing hours?
- Does it support your platform and region?
- Are new and returning players welcome?
- Is voice chat required or optional?
- Are events required or optional?
- Does the fleet focus on casual play, roleplay, endgame builds, competitive damage, or a mixture?
- How do ranks and promotions work?
- Are donations required?
- How is fleet-store access granted?
- Which communication channels does the fleet use?
- What conduct rules apply?

Avoid communities which pressure members to spend money, surrender account information, or donate resources without explaining how access works.

## 3. Your First Steps After Joining

Use this sequence during your first few sessions:

1. Read the welcome message and fleet rules.
2. Learn where announcements and help requests belong.
3. Introduce yourself if the community encourages introductions.
4. Check your fleet rank and current permissions.
5. Review holdings before contributing resources.
6. Ask how store access and promotions work.
7. Join an activity when you feel ready.
8. Request advice before making expensive changes.

Do not feel pressured to prove your value immediately. Reliable participation and respectful communication matter more than a large first donation.

## 4. Understanding Fleet Holdings

Fleet holdings are shared development projects. Members contribute eligible resources to projects, and completed projects improve the holding or support access to fleet services.

Common holding functions include:

- Development projects
- Fleet stores
- Equipment or service unlocks
- Provision production
- Shared progression goals

Before contributing, read the project requirements carefully. Contributions are normally permanent. A fleet contribution is not a personal savings account, and leaving a fleet does not return donated resources.

Choose donations you understand. Keep currencies and materials needed for your own progression.

## 5. Fleet Credits, Provisions, and Stores

Fleet Credits are earned by contributing eligible resources to fleet projects. They are used with other currencies to purchase certain fleet items.

Store access might also depend on:

- Fleet rank
- Store permission
- Available provisions
- Holding development
- A contribution threshold
- A request to an officer

Fleet Credits alone do not guarantee a purchase. Ask before planning around a fleet item.

Before buying:

1. Confirm the item supports your build.
2. Check every required currency.
3. Confirm the store has provisions.
4. Confirm your rank has purchase permission.
5. Compare the item with mission, reputation, event, crafted, and Exchange alternatives.
6. Avoid upgrading the item until you know it belongs in the finished build.

## 6. Ranks, Permissions, and Promotion

Fleet ranks organize permissions. They might control invitations, store purchases, bank access, project management, officer tools, and leadership functions.

Promotion standards vary. They might consider:

- Time in the fleet
- Participation
- Conduct
- Contributions
- Event support
- Communication
- Leadership responsibility

A higher rank is not always a measure of skill. Some fleets keep broad access at lower ranks, while others reserve permissions for administrative reasons.

Never remove fleet-bank items unless you understand the rules. Shared access carries a duty to use resources fairly.

## 7. Events and Communication

Fleet events might include story missions, patrols, Task Force Operations, social gatherings, training, roleplay, or build reviews.

Before an event:

- Check the platform, date, time zone, difficulty, and expected duration.
- Read equipment or level requirements.
- Tell the organizer if you are new to the activity.
- Arrive with injuries or ship damage cleared where relevant.
- Follow the event leader during organized objectives.
- Leave space for questions and different experience levels.

Use the correct communication channel for announcements, build help, spoilers, social discussion, and support requests. Voice chat should follow the fleet's stated policy.

## 8. Good Fleet Conduct

Strong fleet communities depend on predictable conduct.

- Treat members with respect.
- Welcome questions from new and returning players.
- Keep advice constructive.
- Separate personal preference from confirmed game information.
- Credit players who created or tested a build.
- Avoid spoilers unless the discussion permits them.
- Do not pressure members to spend money or resources.
- Follow event-leader instructions.
- Respect privacy and account security.
- Report serious concerns through the fleet's stated process.

Disagreement about builds or play styles does not require personal conflict. Explain the goal, evidence, cost, and tradeoffs behind your advice.

## 9. How to Ask for Useful Help

A focused question receives better answers.

For fleet questions, include:

- Your platform
- Your time zone
- Your normal playing hours
- Your preferred activities
- Whether you use voice chat
- The rule or permission you need explained

For build questions, include:

- Captain faction and career
- Character level
- Ship name and tier
- Weapons and core equipment
- Consoles
- Bridge-officer abilities
- Traits and duty officers
- Specializations
- Preferred play style
- Target difficulty
- Available budget
- Screenshots or a build-planner link

Instead of asking, "What is the best ship?", ask, "What are the three most useful changes for this ship within my current resources?"

## 10. Leaving or Changing Fleets

Sometimes a fleet is not the right fit. Leave respectfully when possible.

Before leaving:

- Finish any responsibility you accepted.
- Return borrowed information or clarify shared work.
- Avoid emptying shared bank resources.
- Save public links you still need.
- Tell an officer if the community expects notice.
- Do not create conflict over ordinary differences in play style.

Choose your next fleet using what you learned about your schedule, preferred content, and communication needs.
