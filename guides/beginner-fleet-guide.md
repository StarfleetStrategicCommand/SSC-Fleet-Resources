# Beginner Fleet Guide

Joining a fleet gives you access to a community, organized activities, advice, and fleet holdings. Each fleet operates differently, so take time to find one matching your play style.

## Before Joining

Ask these questions:

- Is the fleet active during your usual playing hours?
- Does the fleet welcome new or returning players?
- Are events optional?
- How does promotion work?
- Are fleet-store purchases subject to contribution requirements?
- Which communication channels does the fleet use?

## Your First Steps

1. Read the fleet rules and welcome information.
2. Introduce yourself.
3. Ask where help requests and event sign-ups belong.
4. Review fleet holdings before spending resources.
5. Join an activity when you feel ready.
6. Ask questions before making expensive build changes.

## Good Fleet Conduct

- Treat every member with respect.
- Avoid spoilers unless the discussion permits them.
- Follow event-leader instructions during organized activities.
- Do not pressure members to spend money or resources.
- Keep advice constructive.
- Credit the player who created or tested a build.

## Getting Help

When requesting build advice, include:

- Character faction and career
- Ship name and tier
- Current equipment
- Preferred play style
- Available budget
- Content causing difficulty

For SSC information and community support, visit the [SSC Fleet website](https://starfleetstrategiccommand.com/).
